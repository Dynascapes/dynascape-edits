FAQ

Good questions

What do I need to send?

Your raw footage or finished long video as a shared link, plus 1–2 reference videos in the style you want.

How does payment work?

After the free spots, paid edits go through a secure Stripe checkout link. Bigger projects are 50% upfront and 50% on delivery.

What niches do you edit?

Gaming, commentary, news, documentary, podcasts and talking-head content. If it lives on YouTube or TikTok, ask.

What software do you use?

DaVinci Resolve and CapCut. You get a finished MP4 in any format: 9:16 for Shorts, 16:9 for YouTube or 1:1.

How fast is delivery?

A first cut in about 48 hours for Shorts and most YouTube edits. Longer videos are agreed upfront.

Who owns the final video?

You do. We only feature it in this portfolio with your permission.
